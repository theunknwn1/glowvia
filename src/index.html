Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Simply click any of the "WhatsApp" buttons on this page. Our team will chat with you to understand your requirements and process the order.

Can everything be customized?

Yes! We specialize in 100% customization. From the contents and colors to the specific handwritten notes inside.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes, we provide safe and secure Pan India delivery. Shipping timelines vary based on location.

What is the minimum budget?

We try to accommodate various budgets while maintaining our premium quality standard. Message us with your budget, and we'll suggest the best options.

How long does preparation take?

Depending on the customization level, crafting usually takes 2-4 days before dispatch. We recommend ordering at least a week in advance.
